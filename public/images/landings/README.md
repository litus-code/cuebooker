# Landing editorial imagery

Two distinct fictional editorial scenes generated with OpenAI image generation on 14 October 2026 for this redesign. Both were visually reviewed before inclusion: fully clothed adults, industrial music settings, natural faces, no third-party brands or purported customers. They are labelled illustrative in the UI.

DJ: a DJ beside a booth/backstage. Agency: a booking professional with a laptop and papers in a music studio. Originals are retained outside the repository in the cloud session's generated_images directory. Responsive WebP exports are 640 × 800 and 960 × 1200; no animation or remote image dependencies. Full-frame 4:5 rendering preserves faces on mobile.
