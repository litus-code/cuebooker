# CUE ID — Concept Direction V2

Updated: 19 September 2026
Status: WORKING VISUAL REFERENCE — NOT YET PRODUCTION APPROVED

## Purpose

This document translates the V2 asset brief into a visual concept checkpoint before any production 3D asset is authored.

## What the first V2 concept establishes

The first V2 concept successfully moves away from the rejected procedural mannequin toward:

- credible adult human proportions;
- contemporary club/editorial wardrobe;
- professional artist presence;
- three first-class bases;
- shared pose language;
- one common editorial tee;
- restrained dark material language;
- mobile-oriented full-figure framing.

## What is still not approved

The first concept is too close to a fashion-model / photoreal digital-human presentation to be considered the final CUE ID art direction.

Specific risks:

- hair carries too much identity;
- glasses/accessories carry too much personality;
- photoreal skin/detail risks competing with real artist photography rather than complementing it;
- the concept needs a more authored sculptural head language;
- the figure must still feel complete with no accessory and minimal/no hair treatment.

## Concept V2.1 target

The next visual reference must keep the professional/editorial credibility while becoming more recognisably CUE ID.

Required changes:

- no glasses;
- no cap/headphones;
- minimal or absent hairstyle as an identity crutch;
- sculptural facial planes rather than photoreal portrait likeness;
- clear forehead / cheek / jaw / chin authorship;
- subtle eye socket, nose and mouth planes without realistic eyeballs;
- same editorial tee across all bases;
- same neutral pose and lighting across all bases;
- feminine / neutral / masculine differences readable from structure alone;
- same dark graphite palette with restrained lime/red signal only;
- full-body mobile-readable silhouette.

## Evaluation rule

If the three bases cannot be distinguished and feel complete in a no-accessory still, the concept fails.

Accessories and styling may enhance CUE ID later, but they must never rescue a weak base identity.

## Approved working principle

CUE ID should sit between:

- real photography;
- editorial sculpture;
- contemporary fashion campaign CGI;
- music artwork.

It should not sit at either extreme:

- photoreal digital double;
- low-poly avatar.

The desired space is authored, sculptural, human and credible.
