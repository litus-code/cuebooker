# Cuebooker commercial and monetization strategy

Updated: 24 September 2026  
Status: V1 PRODUCT DECISION  
Applies to: initial public launch, Artist product, future Manager / Agency tier

## 1. Commercial thesis

Cuebooker should not sell isolated features. It should monetize the amount of professional operation, automation and team complexity a user needs.

The product loop is:

```text
REQUEST
→ CONVERSATION
→ HOLD
→ DECISION
→ BOOKING
→ EVENT
→ CUE PASSPORT
```

The free product must allow a DJ to experience this loop for real. Paid plans should remove operational limits and add automation, advanced presentation, integrations, reporting and multi-artist management.

Cuebooker should launch with real pricing and payment capability, but should not require payment to start using the product.

## 2. Positioning

Cuebooker is not primarily:

- a generic CRM;
- a ticketing product;
- a marketplace;
- a portfolio builder;
- a social network;
- an AI product.

Cuebooker is an operating system for artist booking work that turns real activity into professional identity and trajectory.

Working commercial promise:

> Manage the work behind your bookings, then let Cuebooker turn that activity into your professional trajectory.

The more concise acquisition promise remains:

> Stop managing bookings across Instagram, WhatsApp, email and notes.

## 3. Launch product

The first public product should show one coherent flow rather than every concept currently under development.

### 3.1 What the user should understand in the first session

The artist should be able to understand that Cuebooker can:

1. receive booking requests from a public link or form;
2. keep conversation and operational context attached to a booking;
3. manage holds, status and decisions;
4. project bookings into the calendar;
5. automate repetitive follow-up without taking the final commercial decision away from the artist;
6. publish a professional artist profile;
7. build CUE PASSPORT automatically from confirmed activity.

CUE ID should be present as an optional identity layer, not as the main reason to adopt Cuebooker.

### 3.2 Launch emphasis

Primary:
- Booking Core
- public booking ingress
- conversation and follow-up
- holds / confirmation / rejection
- calendar
- activity history
- notifications
- public artist profile
- CUE PASSPORT basic trajectory

Secondary:
- CUE ID
- advanced Passport presentation
- media memories
- shareable artifacts

Later:
- public DJ discovery
- promoter / venue network
- marketplace behaviour
- verified cross-party booking history
- wider ecosystem features

## 4. Pricing model

Recommended initial structure:

| Plan | Initial price | Primary user |
|---|---:|---|
| Free | EUR 0 | DJ trying or lightly using Cuebooker |
| Artist Pro | EUR 9.99 / month | Active independent DJ |
| Agency | EUR 39 / month | Manager or small agency |

Recommended annual pricing:

| Plan | Annual price |
|---|---:|
| Artist Pro | EUR 99 / year |
| Agency | EUR 390 / year |

These are launch hypotheses, not permanent prices. Pricing must be reviewed using activation, retention, willingness-to-pay interviews and paid conversion data.

Do not add more than these three plans at launch.

## 5. Free plan

The Free plan exists to let an artist experience the complete Cuebooker loop, not merely view a demo.

Recommended Free scope:

### Included

- 1 artist
- 1 user
- public artist profile
- public booking form and shareable booking link
- basic Booking Core
- basic calendar
- booking status and holds
- basic conversation history
- basic notifications
- CUE PASSPORT creation
- basic CUE PASSPORT constellation / timeline / stickers
- basic CUE ID
- manual booking capture
- limited Smart Capture allowance
- basic distribution links
- QR / share entry surface

### Capacity limits

The launch should prefer capacity limits over feature removal.

Initial limits to test:

- up to 5 active booking processes at one time;
- up to 10 new booking captures per calendar month;
- 90 days of easily browsable operational history;
- limited automated follow-ups;
- limited Smart Capture / AI processing;
- limited Passport media storage;
- Cuebooker branding remains visible.

Exact limits should remain configurable until beta behaviour is understood.

### What must remain free

Do not place the following behind a hard paywall in V1:

- receiving the first booking;
- confirming a booking;
- seeing the booking in calendar;
- publishing the basic artist profile;
- creating the first Passport trajectory;
- basic CUE PASSPORT progression;
- basic booking form;
- basic CUE ID.

These capabilities demonstrate why Cuebooker is different and create long-term product attachment.

## 6. Artist Pro

Artist Pro monetizes repeated professional use.

Recommended scope:

- high or unlimited booking capacity;
- full operational history;
- advanced follow-up automation;
- more Smart Capture / AI allowance;
- advanced notification rules;
- richer CUE PASSPORT;
- linked event media;
- Passport exports and shareable recaps;
- advanced CUE ID presentation options;
- profile customization;
- removal or reduction of Cuebooker branding where appropriate;
- advanced analytics;
- data export;
- advanced distribution / attribution;
- custom domain or custom public URL options when technically ready;
- higher media/storage allowances;
- external integrations when available.

Do not sell individual Passport stickers, individual CUE ID items or single automation actions as microtransactions in V1.

## 7. Agency plan

Agency should monetize multi-artist operation and team coordination.

Recommended entry scope:

- multi-artist workspace;
- multiple team members;
- roles and permissions;
- shared operational inbox;
- roster-level calendar;
- artist switching;
- cross-artist reporting;
- team activity / ownership visibility;
- higher automation and Smart Capture allowances;
- agency-level templates;
- roster-level contacts and counterparties;
- advanced exports;
- priority support once support capacity exists.

Initial EUR 39 / month should be treated as an early small-agency price. As multi-artist depth improves, the plan can move toward a base workspace fee plus included artists and additional roster capacity.

Avoid per-booking transaction fees. Cuebooker should benefit when an artist receives more bookings, not penalize that success.

## 8. Founding Artists offer

Recommended early commercial cohort:

### Founding Artist Pro

- EUR 7.99 / month or EUR 79 / year;
- limited to a defined early cohort, for example the first 100 paying artists;
- price remains while the subscription remains continuously active;
- participants are explicitly told the product is early and their feedback will influence development.

This offer should be used to obtain real willingness-to-pay evidence, not to create artificial urgency.

## 9. Trial policy

Previous Cuebooker strategy assumed a 30-day trial followed by a paid Solo plan. The commercial direction is now freemium.

Recommended behaviour:

- no mandatory trial for Free;
- Free does not expire;
- Artist Pro can optionally offer a short Pro trial later;
- do not require a card to enter Free;
- do not force a subscription before the user has experienced a real booking flow.

The existing non-enforcing `workspace_billing` trial foundation can remain as infrastructure, but entitlement logic should be adapted to support a permanent Free plan before payment enforcement is introduced.

## 10. What Cuebooker should monetize

Monetize operational scale and professional convenience:

- automation volume;
- full history;
- advanced Smart Capture / assistant usage;
- advanced CUE PASSPORT presentation;
- event media volume;
- exports and professional recaps;
- advanced analytics;
- custom branding / domain features;
- integrations;
- collaboration;
- multi-artist operation;
- agency reporting;
- higher storage and processing limits.

## 11. What Cuebooker should not monetize initially

Do not charge separately for:

- the basic booking form;
- the first public profile;
- confirming a booking;
- the calendar itself;
- the existence of CUE PASSPORT;
- individual cities or venues in Passport;
- basic profile links;
- QR entry;
- each booking transaction;
- each promoter enquiry.

Avoid a product experience where the artist encounters payment prompts during the core booking decision flow.

## 12. CUE PASSPORT commercial role

CUE PASSPORT should primarily serve retention and differentiation.

It becomes more valuable as the artist accumulates:

- bookings;
- venues;
- cities;
- countries;
- milestones;
- media;
- professional relationships.

The basic Passport should therefore exist on Free.

Artist Pro can add:

- richer visualization;
- advanced media memories;
- exports;
- shareable yearly recaps;
- extended historical views;
- more customization;
- richer CUE ID integration;
- future verified professional context.

The business goal is not to charge for every Passport object. The goal is for Passport to become increasingly valuable as Cuebooker becomes part of the artist's real working history.

## 13. CUE ID commercial role

CUE ID is optional. A DJ must be able to use Cuebooker professionally without creating an avatar.

Free:
- basic CUE ID creation;
- limited presentation options.

Artist Pro:
- advanced visual customization;
- premium presentation modes;
- richer exports / reusable assets;
- future advanced scenes or renders where processing cost justifies it.

Do not turn CUE ID into a cosmetic-item store during initial product validation.

## 14. AI and automation

AI should not be sold as the product itself.

Cuebooker should sell the outcome:

- less manual data entry;
- less forgotten follow-up;
- cleaner booking context;
- faster capture from messy inbound channels.

Recommended model:

Free:
- small monthly allowance.

Artist Pro:
- materially higher allowance.

Agency:
- workspace-level allowance appropriate to roster size.

Only introduce explicit usage-based charging if actual processing cost becomes commercially meaningful. Measure real cost per active account first.

## 15. Passport Media and external integrations

The Passport Media model should remain source-agnostic:

```text
BOOKING → MEDIA
```

Potential sources:

- manual upload;
- Instagram;
- other authorized social sources;
- future promoter / venue contribution.

External media should be treated as suggested context until confirmed where matching is ambiguous.

Instagram integration should not be required for the core Passport product. Cuebooker should store only the data and media references needed for the product experience and should respect account authorization and platform rules.

## 16. Metrics

Do not optimize revenue before Cuebooker demonstrates repeated product use.

### Acquisition

- visitors to signup;
- signup source;
- role selected;
- artist profile started.

### Activation

The main activation path:

```text
SIGNUP
→ PROFILE PUBLISHED
→ BOOKING ENTRY SHARED
→ FIRST REQUEST
→ FIRST RESPONSE / FOLLOW-UP
→ FIRST CONFIRMED BOOKING
→ FIRST PASSPORT EVENT
```

Core activation metrics:

- percentage publishing a profile;
- percentage sharing a booking link;
- percentage receiving or manually capturing a first booking;
- percentage reaching first confirmed booking;
- median time to first confirmed booking;
- percentage generating first Passport trajectory.

### Retention

Track:

- artists active in week 2;
- artists active in week 4;
- monthly active artists with meaningful booking activity;
- repeat booking capture;
- repeat conversation / follow-up use;
- repeated calendar use;
- Passport return visits after confirmed events.

Avoid using daily logins as the main retention metric. Booking work is naturally irregular.

### Monetization

Track:

- Free → Pro conversion;
- time from signup to upgrade;
- conversion after reaching capacity limit;
- conversion after first confirmed booking;
- conversion after repeated automation usage;
- monthly recurring revenue;
- annual recurring revenue;
- churn;
- plan downgrade rate;
- revenue per paying workspace.

### Cost

Track:

- Smart Capture / AI cost per active user;
- media storage cost;
- email cost;
- notification cost;
- infrastructure cost per active workspace;
- support time per paying account.

## 17. Upgrade triggers

Upgrade messaging should appear when the user encounters real additional value.

Good triggers:

- reaching Free active-booking capacity;
- needing older history;
- configuring repeated automation;
- requesting advanced Passport export;
- exceeding included media allowance;
- adding another artist;
- adding another team member;
- requesting advanced reporting.

Bad triggers:

- opening a booking;
- confirming a booking;
- viewing the calendar;
- receiving the first enquiry;
- viewing the first Passport city.

## 18. Early beta commercial validation

The first external cohort should test product behaviour and payment behaviour simultaneously.

Recommended cohort:

- independent DJs with real booking activity;
- a small number of managers;
- mixture of emerging and established artists;
- users currently coordinating work through WhatsApp, Instagram and email.

For each early user, capture:

- current booking workflow;
- number of monthly enquiries;
- channels used;
- biggest operational pain;
- which Cuebooker capability first felt valuable;
- what they expected to pay before seeing pricing;
- whether they would pay EUR 9.99;
- which capability caused the upgrade decision;
- which missing capability would cause cancellation.

Do not change pricing based on one vocal user. Look for repeated patterns.

## 19. Pricing review criteria

Review Artist Pro pricing after there is enough evidence to answer:

- Are activated Free users retaining?
- Are users hitting capacity limits naturally?
- Which paid capabilities are actually used?
- Is EUR 9.99 perceived as trivial, fair or expensive?
- Does support / infrastructure cost fit the plan?
- Are agencies producing materially more operational value than solo artists?

Possible later outcomes include:

- keeping EUR 9.99 as entry price;
- moving Artist Pro toward EUR 12-15;
- adding a higher professional tier;
- increasing Agency pricing as team features mature.

Do not change pricing merely because competitors charge more.

## 20. Market reference

Market prices are reference points, not a pricing formula.

As checked on 24 September 2026:

- Gigwell publicly lists booking products from USD 39/month and broader agency-oriented products at higher price points.
- Bandzoogle lists musician website plans at USD 11, 17 and 22/month, with EPK-only pricing below those plans.

Cuebooker sits between simple artist-presence tooling and heavier professional booking software. The EUR 9.99 Artist Pro hypothesis intentionally lowers adoption friction while the product earns deeper professional value.

## 21. Product decision summary

For V1:

```text
FREE
Experience the real product.

ARTIST PRO
Remove operational limits and add advanced automation,
history, media, presentation and professional tooling.

AGENCY
Operate multiple artists and a team.
```

Commercial priorities:

1. Make the booking loop excellent.
2. Let Free users experience a real outcome.
3. Charge for scale, automation and professional depth.
4. Let Passport create long-term product attachment.
5. Validate willingness to pay from the beginning.
6. Keep pricing simple until actual behaviour justifies more complexity.
